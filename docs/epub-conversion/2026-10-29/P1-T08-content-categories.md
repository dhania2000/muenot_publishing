# P1-T08 Content category requirements catalogue

Version 1.0 · 29 October 2026 · depends on P1-T07 · documentation only.

Each row specifies a future rights-cleared synthetic test book, not a customer file or an existing completed fixture. The baseline structure includes language, title/creator metadata, logical headings, navigable reading order, stable identifiers and reviewed alternatives. Category differences must be explicit profile data, not another dashboard or converter per category.

| ID and category | Representative source fixture | Semantic/navigation/media requirements | Exception handling and QA acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAT-01 Trade | DOCX novel with front matter, 3 chapters and notes | Chapter hierarchy, scene breaks, notes/backlinks; cover rights | Decorative separators do not become headings; verify chapter/footnote return paths and font enlargement |
| CAT-02 Academic | XML/DOCX monograph with citations/index | Bibliography, citations, cross-references, index/page mapping and tables | Unresolved citations held; every cross-reference resolves and captions remain adjacent |
| CAT-03 STEM | LaTeX/DOCX with fractions, matrices, units and graph | Accessible equations, notation, data tables, figure alternatives and long explanations | Do not flatten math silently or invent graph values; compare symbols/units against source and human review |
| CAT-04 Medical | Structured textbook with dosage/unit tables and diagrams | Preserve exact source values, superscripts, labels and relationships | Recognition ambiguity blocks affected content; domain reviewer compares every critical value; no medical correctness claim inferred |
| CAT-05 Journals | JATS issue with article authors, abstracts and references | Article boundaries/IDs, affiliations, citations, permissions and supplementary links | Mixed metadata and broken references block release; test article-level navigation and contributor attribution |
| CAT-06 Educational | Lesson with exercises, answers, diagrams and audio | Objectives, question/answer relations, accessible interactions and transcript | Answer leakage and inaccessible activities flagged; keyboard path plus static alternative must be reviewed |
| CAT-07 Dictionaries | XML headwords with pronunciation, senses and cross-links | Searchable text, lexical hierarchy, Unicode and language switches | Font/diacritic loss blocks affected entries; verify linked headwords, accents and pronunciation alternatives |
| CAT-08 Children's | Illustrated fixed-layout story with read-aloud | Text reading order, non-text descriptions, synchronized narration and spreads | Missing narration transcript or image-only text held; review single-page and spread ordering |
| CAT-09 Comics | Panel sequence with balloons and sound effects | Panel/balloon order, meaningful text alternatives, character attribution grounded in source | Ambiguous panel order requires editor decision; reading sequence must make sense without page geometry |
| CAT-10 Manga | RTL panel sample with mixed text direction | Binding direction, panel order and isolated LTR segments | No mirrored artwork or reordered text; named-reader page progression and assistive order tested |

| CAT-12 Photography | Rights-cleared photo essay with captions/credits | Image quality, sequence, captions, credits and meaningful descriptions | Avoid autogenerated identities or facts; rights and description approval plus zoom quality review |

| CAT-14 Interactive | HTML lesson with a scripted exercise/video | Inert default import, declared interaction profile, keyboard semantics, captions/transcripts | Unsupported scripting cannot receive a pass; provide static equivalent and test supported reader sandbox behavior |

## Acceptance and open dependencies

CAT-01–CAT-14 cover every requested category with sample design, exception route and measurable review criteria. Cross-cutting rights/licensing, language, navigation, formulas, complex tables and accessible alternatives are explicit. Specification acceptance is met; fixture execution and subject/reader sign-off remain future work. Category labels are organizational requirements, not claims of installed engines.
